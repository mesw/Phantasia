### 2.2 The Apple Test (Step 1: Intuitive Self-Assessment)
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Step one: The Apple Test. <break time="1.0s" /> Find a relaxed, seated position with both feet resting gently on the floor. When you are ready, gently close your eyes. <break time="2.0s" /> In your mind, consider the concept of a red apple resting on a wooden tabletop. <break time="2.0s" /> Observe what occurs within your subjective awareness. <break time="1.5s" /> Do you perceive an inner visual rendering—vivid and luminous, complete with glossy skin and stem? <break time="1.5s" /> Do you detect a muted, faint silhouette? <break time="1.5s" /> Or is your inner space completely dark, yet you know and understand with certainty that an apple is being thought of? <break time="2.0s" /> Open your eyes, and select the illustration on screen that most faithfully matches your direct experience."

---

### 2.3 Standardized VVIQ Evaluation (Step 2: Marks 1973)
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Step two: The Visual Imagery Questionnaire. <break time="1.0s" /> Across the following sixteen items, you will be invited to contemplate four familiar scenarios. For each specific detail, rate the vividness of your mental impression on a scale from one to five. <break time="1.5s" /> One represents complete absence of an image—no picture at all, only the conceptual knowledge that you are thinking of the object. Five represents absolute vividness—as clear, vibrant, and detailed as normal vision. <break time="1.5s" /> There are no right or wrong ratings. Rate only your genuine, immediate inner impression."

### 2.4 Multi-Sensory Modality Profiling (Step 3: Dawes et al. 2024)
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Step three: Multi-Sensory Profiling. <break time="1.0s" /> Cognitive imagery is not limited to the visual stream. ### WEEK 1: Multi-Modal Anchoring & Sensory Scaffolding

#### Day 1: Tactile Blueprinting
- **Scientific Foundation:** Somatosensory frontoparietal scaffolding (Dawes et al. 2024; Kelly 2020).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day one: Tactile Blueprinting. <break time="1.0s" /> Pick up a small, textured physical object—a metallic coin, an embossed key, or a textured stone. With your eyes open, spend sixty seconds tracing its contours, edge ridges, surface temperatures, and weight in your fingertips. <break time="2.0s" /> Now, gently close your eyes. <break time="1.5s" /> Do not attempt to force a visual photograph in your mind. Instead, recreate purely the tactile blueprint: the cold hardness of the metal, the bite of the sharp edge against your index finger, the pressure resting in your palm. <break time="2.0s" /> Feel the spatial truth of the object through your somatosensory memory. Hold this physical anchor steady."

#### Day 2: Spatial Coordinate Mapping
- **Scientific Foundation:** Visuospatial coordinate independence (Monzel 2025; Jacobs et al. 2018).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day two: Spatial Coordinate Mapping. <break time="1.0s" /> Today, we construct a three-by-three spatial grid in the space directly before your chest. <break time="1.5s" /> Mentally assign three locations: In the upper-left, anchor a book. In the exact center, anchor a tall glass of water. In the lower-right, anchor a heavy brass key. <break time="2.0s" /> Without needing to visualize color or light, reach out with your mental attention to the upper-left coordinate. Feel the distance and angle. Now shift attention diagonally through the center glass down to the key. <break time="2.0s" /> Notice that spatial relationships exist clearly in your mind without any visual pixels."

#### Day 3: Acoustic Resonance Anchors
- **Scientific Foundation:** Auditory imagery preservation & cross-priming (Dawes et al. 2024).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day three: Acoustic Resonance Anchors. <break time="1.0s" /> Close your eyes and recall a clear, rhythmic acoustic sound: the steady ticking of an analog clock, or raindrops tapping against a windowpane. <break time="2.0s" /> Replay the timbre, the sharp transient attack, and the resonant decay of each beat. <break time="1.5s" /> Now notice where the sound is situated in space relative to your ears. Allow the acoustic precision to act as a reliable anchor for mental focus."

#### Day 4: Thermal & Weight Invariance
- **Scientific Foundation:** Insular & somatosensory cortex recruitment (Rhodes et al. 2024).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day four: Thermal and Weight Invariance. <break time="1.0s" /> Rest both hands comfortably open on your lap. <break time="1.0s" /> In your left palm, mentally place a cold ice cube. Feel the freezing dampness melting and cooling your skin. <break time="2.0s" /> In your right palm, place a warm ceramic mug of tea. Feel the comforting radiant warmth penetrating your fingers. <break time="2.0s" /> Alternate your conscious attention: left hand intense cold, right hand soothing warmth. Observe how vivid physical sensations can become without any visual image."

#### Day 5: Semantic Feature Association
- **Scientific Foundation:** Propositional networks as compensatory scaffolding (Monzel 2025).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day five: Semantic Feature Association. <break time="1.0s" /> Choose a common fruit, such as an apple. <break time="1.0s" /> Instead of straining for a picture, methodically assemble its propositional facts: Its geometry is a sphere with a top and bottom dimple. Its material is organic, waxy skin. Its weight is approximately one hundred and eighty grams. Its aroma is crisp and sweet. Its bite produces a sharp acoustic crunch. <break time="2.0s" /> Notice that your concept is complete and robust, built upon solid semantic knowledge."

#### Day 6: Dual-Modal Cross-Priming
- **Scientific Foundation:** Multi-sensory binding in supramodal cortices (Dawes et al. 2024).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day six: Dual-Modal Cross-Priming. <break time="1.0s" /> Today we bind sound and touch together. Imagine writing on a dry blackboard with a stick of white chalk. <break time="1.5s" /> Feel the dry chalk dust between your fingertips and the friction dragging across the slate. Simultaneously, mentally hear the tap and squeak of the chalk as you draw a circle. <break time="2.0s" /> Experience the synchronization of touch and sound in your mind."

#### Day 7: Week 1 Integration & Consolidation
- **Scientific Foundation:** Synaptic consolidation and fatigue prevention (Rhodes et al. 2024).
- **Exercise Mode:** `spatial_tactile`
> **Spoken Audio Script:**  
> "Day seven: Week One Integration. <break time="1.0s" /> Reflect on the sensory modalities you explored this week: tactile touch, spatial coordinates, acoustic resonance, and thermal sensations. <break time="1.5s" /> Choose the single sensory anchor that felt clearest to you. Spend the next eight minutes resting comfortably, holding that anchor steady for thirty-second intervals. <break time="2.0s" /> Appreciate the clarity and richness of your non-visual cognitive mind."
