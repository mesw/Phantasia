Research and Educational Instrument — Not a Medical Device
Empirical self-exploration platform evaluating the visual imagery spectrum (from Afantasie / Aphantasia to Hyperfantasie) via VVIQ (Marks 1973) and offering a progressive 6-week neurocognitive exercise curriculum (Rhodes et al. 2024).
100% Client-Side WebAssembly: All evaluations and logs remain strictly in private browser storage (IndexedDB / localStorage).
